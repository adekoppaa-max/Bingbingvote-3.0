🎵 BGM Playlist Manager

One track per line. Use direct audio links (.mp3, .m4a, .ogg, .wav). Optional title: Title | https://.... Tracks switch automatically and visitors can tap Next. Leave empty to use the built-in cute tunes. Streaming pages such as YouTube links will not play.

ℹ️ Supported formats & how to get a direct link
  • Formats: .mp3, .m4a, .ogg (and .wav). The link must open or download the audio file itself, not a web page.
  • Catbox.moe (easiest): upload your file at catbox.moe and paste the link that looks like https://files.catbox.moe/abc123.mp3.
  • Google Drive: share the file as "Anyone with the link", then paste the normal share link. It is converted automatically to a direct download link. Large files may still be blocked by Google.
  • Dropbox: paste the share link. It is converted automatically by changing dl=0 to raw=1.
  • Not supported: YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, and other streaming pages.
  • Quick test: open the link in a new browser tab. If it plays or downloads as audio, it works here. Browsers only start sound after a visitor taps the 🎵 button.

How to order 💌

  1. Open the Shop or pick from the featured apps on Home. Choose a package rate, type the total units you want (for example 40000), then tap Add to Cart.
  2. Open the cart. Pick IDR or USD, choose your payment method, and enter your Twitter handle and any notes.
  4. Tap Checkout via Twitter DM. Your order text is copied and a DM to @bingbingvote opens.
  5. Paste the text in the DM box and send it.
  6. We reply with payment details. Pay, send your proof, and we start voting right away.
  7. Every completed order earns a 🐝 stamp. Check your card in Loyalty and use your voucher in the cart at 10 stamps.
  8. Loved it? Leave a review in Testimonials 💛
